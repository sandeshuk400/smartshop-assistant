About us: Avantika Provisions is a provisions shop in Nagpur. You can order online and get your items delivered to your address, or visit the shop in person.

Shop location and address: Avantika Provisions is located at 123 Sample Street, Sample Nagar, Nagpur - 440000. Landmark: Near Sample Landmark. Find us on Google Maps: https://maps.example.com/avantika-provisions . You can also visit the shop in person during opening hours: Monday to Saturday 9 AM to 6 PM, Sunday 9 AM to 3 PM. Shop address is also called store location, directions, or how to reach us.

Local delivery area: We deliver all over Nagpur, in both urban and rural areas. For other locations, please contact support.

Shipping time: Standard shipping takes 2 to 5 working days. Express shipping delivers within 1 day. Delivery time is also called delivery date or how long it takes to arrive.

Shipping cost: Standard shipping is free on orders above Rs 500. For orders below Rs 500, the delivery charge is up to Rs 50. Express shipping costs Rs 200.

Shipping outside India: Yes, we ship outside India. The charges vary depending on how far the delivery location is from our shop. Please contact support to get the exact charge for your location.

Return policy: You can return an eligible item within 3 working days of delivery. Working days are Monday to Saturday, and Sundays are not counted. Some items cannot be returned. See the entry "Items that cannot be returned". If an item arrived damaged, spoiled, expired, or wrong, see the entry "Damaged or wrong item".

Items that cannot be returned: Edible products cannot be returned. This includes food items such as biscuits, snacks, rice, flour, oil, spices, tea and sweets. Items priced below Rs 100 also cannot be returned. If an item is edible or costs less than Rs 100, the answer to "can I return it" is no.

How to return an item: Open your order and tap the Return of Item button. Our team will then arrange the return.

Refunds: Your refund is processed within 1 day. This is also called getting your money back.

Cash on delivery refunds: For cash on delivery orders, the refund takes 5 to 7 working days.

Payment methods: We accept online payment, UPI, and cash on delivery.

Cash on delivery limit: Cash on delivery is available for orders up to Rs 10000. There is no extra fee for cash on delivery.

Cancelling an order: You can cancel your order whenever you want, before it is delivered. After delivery, use the return process instead.

Changing the delivery address: You can change the delivery address before the delivery person leaves our shop with your order. After that, the address cannot be changed.

Order status in chat: To check the status of an order, send your order number and customer ID in one message, for example: Where is my order 123? My customer ID is 45.

Contact support: Email : support@example.com or call on +91-0000000000. Support hours are Monday to Saturday 9 AM to 6 PM, and Sunday 9 AM to 3 PM.
